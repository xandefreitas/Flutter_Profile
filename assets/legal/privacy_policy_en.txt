Depending on how you use the App, I may collect or process the following information:

The App provides authentication through:

* Phone authentication; and
* Anonymous authentication.

If you authenticate using your phone number, your phone number may be processed in order to create and maintain your account.

If you use anonymous authentication, the App may create an anonymous account identifier associated with your session or account.

### 1.2 Information you provide

If you choose to interact with the App, you may provide information such as:

* Display name;
* Phone number;
* A written deposition, testimonial, or recommendation about my professional profile; and
* Information associated with your account.

You are not required to provide a deposition or testimonial simply to browse the App.

### 1.3 Information generated through your use of the App

The App may record information necessary to provide its functionality, such as:

* Your selected language;
* Your account or authentication identifier;
* Skills you upvote; and
* Information associated with content you submit.

The App may also rely on technical information processed automatically by third-party services used to operate the App, such as authentication, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or security services, if those services are enabled.

I may use the information described above to:
